Speakers:

ANNA: Lufthansa Operations Control agent. Calm, focused, in a hurry. Speaks a little clipped.
MARCO: Sheraton Frankfurt Airport front desk manager. Friendly, professional, slightly rushed.

Setting: Anna is calling on behalf of 84 stranded passengers. Late evening. Background: airport noise on her side (optional, keeps it realistic).

[phone ring, click]

MARCO: Sheraton Frankfurt Airport, front desk, Marco speaking. How can I help?

ANNA: Hi Marco, this is Anna from Lufthansa Operations Control at Frankfurt. Urgent situation. Flight LH 123 to New York has been cancelled tonight, technical issue. We have 84 passengers who need overnight accommodation starting immediately. Can you help us?

MARCO: 84 rooms tonight, that's a large block. Let me check availability. [pause 2 seconds] OK, we do have 84 standard rooms available, but I need you to commit fast, because we have another airline that may also call tonight.

ANNA: Understood. What is your rate?

MARCO: For emergency same-day bookings the rate is 119 euros per room, per night. That includes breakfast and VAT. On top of that there is a flat 500 euro block fee for the last-minute booking.

ANNA: 119 including breakfast and VAT, plus 500 for the block. Got it. Breakfast, what time does it start? Some of our passengers have very early rebookings.

MARCO: Breakfast from five in the morning, no extra charge. It's included.

ANNA: Good. Transport to the terminal?

MARCO: We have a shuttle. For your group I can arrange a dedicated shuttle, 350 euros total, both directions between the hotel and Terminal 1.

ANNA: OK. Cancellation policy? If we release rooms because passengers make their own arrangements, what happens?

MARCO: You can release rooms without charge until 22:00 tonight. Well, ideally by 21:00, because after that our night team takes over and processing gets slow, but officially 22:00 is the deadline. After 22:00 the block is committed and non-refundable, one night per room.

ANNA: So free until 22:00, hard deadline?

MARCO: 22:00 hard, 21:00 preferred.

ANNA: OK. Late check-in? Some passengers may arrive after midnight.

MARCO: Normally we charge 15 euros per room after 23:00 for late arrival, but for you tonight we waive that. No late check-in fee.

ANNA: Excellent. Payment terms?

MARCO: We invoice Lufthansa Operations directly, standard 30-day terms. Send me the rooming list to reservations at sheraton-fra dot com within the hour and we're set.

ANNA: Let me confirm. 84 rooms at 119 euros including breakfast and VAT, plus 500 euro block fee, plus 350 euros shuttle, late check-in waived, cancellation free until 22:00, invoice to Lufthansa Operations, 30 day terms. I send you the rooming list within the hour. Correct?

MARCO: Correct. I'm blocking the rooms now under Lufthansa 123 diversion. Send the list, I'll confirm receipt.

ANNA: Perfect. Thank you Marco. I'll come back to you.

MARCO: You're welcome Anna. Talk soon.

Production notes

Speak naturally, not read. Small hesitations ("uh", "let me see") make Whisper sound more like a real call and make the demo more credible.
Aim for 2:30 to 3:30. Under 2 minutes reads as scripted, over 4 gets boring.
Record on one phone with the other person on speaker, or use a video-call recording (Zoom, Meet), or two people in one room with one recorder in the middle. Save as MP3 or M4A.
