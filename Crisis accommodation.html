How this number was reached
  • Ranking is arithmetic in code, not a model judgement. The same offers always produce the same winner.
  • Coach cost is an assumption: 300 EUR per coach plus 8 EUR per km, 3 coaches for 121 guests.
  • An incomplete reply is never ranked. Missing fields become a specific question, not a guess.
  • Every number the model extracts is checked against the reply text it came from. A value that does not appear in the source is treated as ungrounded and the offer is pulled out of the ranking for a human to read.
  • Splitting the requirement across two hotels purely to keep each piece under the commitment limit is limit avoidance. This workflow will not do it.
What this workflow is not allowed to do
  • This workflow requests offers, reads them and ranks them. It does not book, does not confirm, and does not give any hotel a payment guarantee.
  • No passenger data crosses into this workflow. It receives room counts and times only.
  • Nothing is sent to a real address. The dispatch step is simulated and labelled as such.
